01 ABOUT THE GAME

Learn to think
like a defender.

CyberShield is a single-player educational game where players protect a simulated digital system. Malware arrives in waves; players recognize the threats and place cybersecurity defenses to respond.

05 RESEARCH SNAPSHOT

What the study explored

The capstone examined reported learning responses and learners’ acceptance of CyberShield.

KNOWLEDGE ASSESSMENT01 / 02
05participants

Pre-test / post-test

Post-game responses were generally more favorable across the assessed learning areas. This small assessment group is an early indication, not a broad measure of effectiveness.

Malware awarenessThreat recognitionDefense awarenessThreat / defense decisions
GAME ACCEPTANCE02 / 02
46respondents

Extended Technology Acceptance Model

Responses were generally favorable across enjoyment, ease of use, usefulness, and intention to use the game for learning.

Perceived enjoymentEase of useUsefulnessIntention to use

Keep the groups distinct. The knowledge assessment had 5 participants; the acceptance evaluation had 46 respondents. Positive intention to use reflects willingness, not proof of actual long-term use.
